## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-12-01 | Tampering | Security::setLocale | mitigate | whitelist `in_array($locale, config('App')->supportedLocales, true)`, inak 422 bez UPDATE; hodnota sa nikdy nepoužije ako cesta k súboru |
| T-12-02 | Tampering (CSRF) | POST /settings/security/locale | mitigate | globálny CSRF filter + `csrf_field()`; test bez tokenu je odmietnutý |
| T-12-03 | Elevation | trasa locale | mitigate | trasa za session filtrom ako ostatné settings/security trasy; test anonymného POST → /login |
| T-12-04 | Repudiation | zmena jazyka | mitigate | `AuditLog::record(AuthEvent::LocaleChanged, ...)` s novou hodnotou v detaile |
| T-12-05 | Tampering (open redirect) | PRG | mitigate | redirect na pevnú trasu `/settings/security`, bez parametra z požiadavky |
| T-12-06 | Information disclosure | Accept-Language | accept | negotiator vyberie iba zo supportedLocales, neznáma hodnota → sk; žiadne citlivé dáta |
</threat_model>
