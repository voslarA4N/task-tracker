`App\Libraries\Work\TaskQueries` (konštruktor `?BaseConnection $db = null`):
- `list(TaskFilter $f, int $limit = 200): array{rows: list<array>, truncated: bool}` — **jeden** `SELECT` z `tasks t JOIN clients c ON c.id = t.client_id JOIN task_billing_v v ON v.task_id = t.id` s podmienkami filtra, `LIMIT :limit + 1`; riadok: `id, title, url, client_id, client_name, currency, hours, rate, quoted_amount` (`round(t.hours * t.rate, 2)`), `status, done_at, created_at, billing_state, billed_hours, planned_hours, allocated_hours, locked_count, unlocked_count`; zoradenie: najprv nezapracované a čiastočné, potom zapracované (`ORDER BY (t.status = 'done' AND v.billing_state <> 'partial'), t.created_at DESC, t.id DESC`, UI-SPEC [predpoklad]); v PHP doplní `remaining` (`Hours::remaining`), `billed_pct` a `planned_pct` (`Hours::percentOf` voči `hours`, súčet orezaný na 100).
- `detail(int $id): ?array` — úloha + klient (`client_name`, `currency`, `portal_name`, `client_archived`) + agregáty z `task_billing_v` + `quoted_amount`; `allocations` = časti úlohy `ORDER BY billing_month, id` (`id, hours, billing_month, rate, amount, currency, invoice_id, locked`); odvodené `remaining`, `delta` (`Hours::delta(allocated, hours)`), `over_quoted` (allocated > hours), `has_locked`, `billed_pct`, `planned_pct`. Najviac dva dotazy (úloha, časti), null pre neexistujúce id.
- `dashboard(string $month, ?int $clientId): array` — konštantný počet dotazov (najviac 5) bez ohľadu na počet úloh:
  - `due` = `SELECT a.currency, SUM(a.amount) AS amount, SUM(a.hours) AS hours FROM billing_allocations a JOIN tasks t ... WHERE a.invoice_id IS NULL AND a.billing_month = :month: [AND t.client_id = :client:] GROUP BY a.currency ORDER BY a.currency`;
  - `wip` = `SELECT c.currency, SUM(round(t.hours * t.rate, 2)) AS amount, SUM(t.hours) AS hours FROM tasks t JOIN clients c ... WHERE t.status = 'in_progress' [AND t.client_id] GROUP BY c.currency` (súčet zaokrúhlených súm úloh, nie zaokrúhlenie súčtu; D-14: nacenené nezapracované úlohy, RESEARCH Open Question 1);
  - `groups.done_due` = zapracované úlohy s nezamknutou časťou v `month` (max 10 riadkov + `total`), `groups.partial` = `billing_state = 'partial'` (max 10 + `total`, s `billed_pct`, `planned_pct`), `groups.in_progress` = `status = 'in_progress'` (max 10 + `total`); riadky rovnakého tvaru ako `list`. Počty cez `COUNT(*) OVER ()` v tom istom dotaze.
